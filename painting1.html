The Starry Night Saint Rémy (1889)

vincent van gogh

The Starry Night, often called simply Starry Night, is an oil-on-canvas painting by the Dutch Post-Impressionistpainter Vincent van Gogh. Painted in June 1889, it depicts the view from the east-facing window of his asylum room at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, just before sunrise, with the addition of an imaginary village
